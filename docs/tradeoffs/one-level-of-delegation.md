# One level of delegation in a chain

**Gain:** a cell delegates without blocking, and `rika.agents.inspectAll` waits up to a `waitMillis` the host clamps, so a parent can outlive the child it delegated to and use the answer. The wait ends as soon as every named child is terminal, and one that elapses reports the children as they are rather than failing.

**Cost:** the real bound is how many Runs hold a scheduler slot at once, and it is smaller than it looks. Baton's local scheduler runs at most `concurrency` Runs, four by default. A spawned child is moved to `running` the instant it is admitted rather than queued for later, a parent holds its slot for its whole turn because nothing returns it to `queued` while a child is unsettled, a parent that waits holds its slot for the whole wait, and a child that calls a tool before it answers holds one across both of its turns. A Thread's first turn also admits a Title agent. So a root that waits, its Title, and two children are already four of four, and so is a root that waits on one child which uses a tool and then answers. Whenever the last turn of that arithmetic needs a slot an outer Run is holding until precisely that turn arrives, the chain cannot finish: the inner agent stops, its cell stays `running`, any outer wait spends its whole budget, and nothing reports it. Waiting does not cause this and removing the wait does not avoid it — a parent that only admits its children races them instead. Delegation is dependable when the delegating parent, its Title, and the children it waits for fit inside the concurrency, counting a tool-using child twice; deeper or wider than that, an outer level must return and read results from the durable transcript instead.

**Rejected:** raising the scheduler's concurrency moves the width and depth at which the chain stalls rather than removing the limit, and the scheduler admits oldest-first, so added capacity reaches the shallowest Runs before the deepest one that is actually blocked. Releasing a slot for the duration of a wait is the real fix and belongs with Baton's execution host rather than a binding, because a Run that yields its slot must reclaim it without losing its cell's live namespace. Blocking admission instead of polling would put a child's latency inside the parent's cell deadline, which is the failure the bounded wait exists to avoid.
